## ENCLAVE 1 — Hardware Sovereignty & Zero-Waste NdFeB Magnet Recycling

High-efficiency sintered permanent magnets of the **NdFeB** system are heterogeneous multiphase structures in which key functional characteristics are determined by the spatial distribution and microstructural interaction of two main phases:

1. **Ferromagnetic matrix phase** (Φ-Nd₂Fe₁₄B) with tetragonal crystal lattice (space group P4₂/mnm), comprising **85–87%** of the total magnet volume. Provides high saturation magnetisation.
2. **Non-magnetic grain boundary phase (GBP)**, enriched with neodymium (Nd-rich phase), comprising **10–12%** of the volume and localised at grain junctions of the main phase. Provides magnetic isolation of grains, preventing nucleation of reverse domains and increasing coercive force (H_ci).

### 1.1. Physical-Chemical Mechanism of Hydrogen Decrepitation (HD) and HDDR Processing

Direct zero-waste recycling of sintered NdFeB permanent magnets is based on **Hydrogen Decrepitation (HD)** and subsequent thermochemical **Hydrogenation-Disproportionation-Desorption-Recombination (HDDR)** cycle, implemented within the **HPMS** (Hydrogen Processing of Magnetic Scrap) technology.

**HD Stage:** Source magnet scrap is exposed to pure H₂ gas at 25–150°C and 0.1–1.0 MPa. The Nd-rich GBP selectively absorbs hydrogen:

$$\text{Nd} + \frac{2.7}{2}\text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{NdH}_{2.7} \quad (\Delta H < 0)$$

Volume expansion differences (GBP +16.1%, matrix +4.8%) generate internal stresses > 2 GPa, cracking the monolith into powder with matrix particle size > 5 μm and GBP particles < 1 μm.

**Disproportionation Stage** (750–950°C, 0.1 MPa H₂):

$$\text{Nd}_2\text{Fe}_{14}\text{B} + (2\pm y)\text{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NdH}_{2\pm y} + 12\alpha\text{-Fe} + \text{Fe}_2\text{B}$$

**Desorption & Recombination Stage** (800–900°C, residual pressure < 10⁻¹ Pa):

$$2\text{Nd} + 12\text{Fe} + \text{Fe}_2\text{B} \rightarrow \text{Nd}_2\text{Fe}_{14}\text{B}$$
