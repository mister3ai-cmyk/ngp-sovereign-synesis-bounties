## Abstract

Subtropical coastal megastructures face severe thermodynamic penalties from conventional compression-based HVAC (>65% of peak urban grid load). This paper presents a closed-loop thermodynamic architecture combining:
- **SWAC**: Deep seawater intake at T ≤ 6.0°C from 600–800 m depth in the Gulf of Oman (COP = 19.8)
- **Venturi Badgirs**: 3.5× contraction ratio accelerating wind to 17.5 m/s, yielding ΔT = −11.2°C across 2.5 km² footprint
- **MED**: Waste heat from compute nodes (55–65°C) drives desalination at 1.8 kWh/m³
